## Make market decisions your AI agent can act on

Klinko turns fragmented market intelligence into a ranked decision: **who to serve, what they need, where the opportunity is, and what to validate next**. It then keeps watching the market so your decision can change when the signals do.

| Discover | Compare | Decide | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Surface audiences, needs, pain points, and emerging opportunities. | Compare demand, buying motivation, competitive gaps, and reachability. | Rank what deserves attention and recommend the next validation step. | Track meaningful shifts in demand, competition, language, and audience structure. |

## What you can do with Klinko

- Find and prioritize target audiences
- Discover niche and early-adopter segments
- Build evidence-led buyer personas
- Analyze customer pain points and buying motivation
- Validate startup ideas and product opportunities
- Develop positioning, messaging, and content direction
- Identify creative angles and viral content patterns
- Monitor when a market decision needs to be revisited
